Privacy Policy

Daily Scripture Poster, operated by Dale, individual developer
Effective date: October 7, 2026

What this app is

Daily Scripture Poster is a private desktop tool used by its owner, Dale, to publish his own short devotional and Bible verse videos to its own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

Information we collect

  • TikTok account information: when the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives basic profile information (such as display name and account ID) and access tokens that allow it to upload videos.
  • Videos and captions: the videos, captions and hashtags the account owner chooses to publish.

We do not collect information about TikTok viewers, followers or any other users.

How we use it

  • To upload and publish videos to the connected TikTok account at the owner's direction.
  • To show the owner whether a post succeeded.

Storage and security

Access tokens and account information are stored only on the account owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Dale, individual developer or any third party, other than TikTok itself when posting.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Information is sent to TikTok only as needed to publish videos through TikTok's official API.

Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions. Disconnecting revokes the app's access. Locally stored tokens can be deleted from the app at any time.

Children

This app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
